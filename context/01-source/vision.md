# Vision

A single, simple management system for the **Society of Twelve** — a Bengali community
organization ("সমাজ, ঐক্য ও উন্নয়ন"). It gives a small set of admins one place to manage
members, collect and receipt monthly fees, track income/expenses, and publish notices,
projects and mahfil activity to a public site. Built now because the society currently runs
these on paper/spreadsheets and needs a shared, auditable, always-available record.

## North-star outcome
Every member fee collected is recorded with a receipt and reflected in finance totals, and
every member/notice the public should see is published — with zero paper ledgers.

## Non-goals
- Not a general-purpose CRM or accounting suite.
- Not a member-facing mobile app (web only for now).
- Not a payment gateway — payments are recorded, not processed online.
