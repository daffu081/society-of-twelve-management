# Business requirements

- BR1: Only trusted, explicitly-authorized people (active admins) may see or change society data.
- BR2: Executive admins get only the capabilities the Super Admin grants them (checkbox permissions).
- BR3: Every fee payment produces a receipt with a unique number the member can be shown.
- BR4: Money figures must be exact — no rounding drift in totals.
- BR5: A member's private details are never public unless the member consents (public-directory toggle).
- BR6: Deleting anything is reversible for 30 days (mistakes must not be permanent).
- BR7 (planned): Members can receive SMS (fee reminders, birthday greetings) once a provider is contracted.
