# Business vision

**Who pays / who runs it:** the Society of Twelve itself — a volunteer community organization.
There is no external customer; the "buyers" are the society's executive committee who need
trustworthy books and a public face.

**Problem:** fee collection, finance and member records live on paper and in individuals'
heads, so nothing is auditable and continuity depends on specific people.

**Success looks like:** the committee can, at any time, answer "who paid what", "what's our
balance", and "who are our members" from one system, and the community can see notices and
activity publicly.
