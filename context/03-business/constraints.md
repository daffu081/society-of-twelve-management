# Business constraints

- Volunteer capacity: features must be operable by non-technical committee members.
- Cost-sensitive: prefer free tiers; SMS/OTP deferred until the org commits to paying a provider.
- Trust/reputation: mishandling member personal data (NID/passport) or finances is unacceptable — favor auditability and consent over convenience.
- Bengali-first for anything the wider community sees.
